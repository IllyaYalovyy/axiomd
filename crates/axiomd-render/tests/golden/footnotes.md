# Footnotes

The order the reader sees is the order of first reference[^second], not the order
the definitions are written in[^first].

A footnote referred to twice[^second] is one footnote with two ways back.

[^first]: Written first, referred to second.

[^second]: Written second, referred to first.

    A second paragraph of the same definition.

[^unused]: Nothing refers to this one.
